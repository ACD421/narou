WHAT THIS IS
----------------------------------------------------------------
Narou matches your resume against 53,000+ live job postings from
Greenhouse and Lever (the ATS platforms used by most tech
companies). It ranks jobs by how well they fit your skills,
factors in posting freshness, flags suspicious ghost postings,
grades companies on hiring trustworthiness, and suggests resume
improvements.

Everything runs locally on your computer. No resume data is
uploaded anywhere. No API keys are needed.

You can also use the hosted version at:
https://teamnarou.streamlit.app


REQUIREMENTS (local install only)
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Windows 10+, macOS 12+, or Linux
- Python 3.10 or later (download from python.org if needed)
- Internet connection (for pip install + first-run index build)


STEP BY STEP SETUP (local install)
----------------------------------------------------------------

Step 1: Download and unzip the project folder

   Get the narou folder onto your computer (USB, zip, or share).
   Put it somewhere easy to find, like Desktop or Documents.

Step 2: Open the narou folder

   You should see files like app.py, install.bat, run.bat,
   install.sh, run.sh, and folders narou/, models/, data/.

Step 3: Run the installer for your OS

   Windows:  double-click install.bat
   macOS:    open Terminal, cd into the folder, then run:
                 chmod +x install.sh run.sh
                 ./install.sh
   Linux:    same as macOS

   The installer will:
   - Verify you have Python 3.10+
   - Create a Python virtual environment (.venv folder)
   - Upgrade pip and clear any stale index cache
   - Install all required packages (streamlit, numpy, etc.)
   - Create the data directories

   This takes 1 to 3 minutes depending on your internet speed.
   When you see "Setup complete!", you are done.

   You only need to do this once.

Step 4: Start the app

   Windows:  double-click run.bat
   macOS:    ./run.sh
   Linux:    ./run.sh

   A terminal window will open and stay open. After a few
   seconds, your default web browser will open automatically
   with the Narou interface.

   If the browser does not open, manually go to:
   http://localhost:8501

Step 5: Wait for the first-run index build

   On the very first launch, Narou decompresses a bundled
   corpus of 53,000+ jobs (takes about 2 seconds) and builds
   a search index (takes about 1 to 2 minutes). You will see
   "Loading match index..." during this process.

   You can upload your resume while the index builds. Once it
   finishes and the sidebar shows a job count (e.g. "Jobs:
   53,760"), you are ready to analyze.


HOW TO USE THE APP
----------------------------------------------------------------

Step 1: Upload your resume

   In the left sidebar under "1. Your resume", click the upload
   area and select a PDF or DOCX file. The app will parse it
   and show how many sections and skills it found.

Step 2: (Optional) Add a focus

   Under "2. Focus", type a short phrase to tilt the ranking
   toward specific interests. Examples:
   - "remote senior backend python"
   - "offensive security red team"
   - "UX research healthcare"
   - "entry level data analyst"

   Leave this blank to let your resume speak for itself.

Step 3: Click Analyze

   Under "4. Analyze", click the Analyze button. The app
   will rank your resume against all 53,000+ jobs in the
   corpus. This takes about 1 to 3 seconds.

Step 4: Review your results

   The main area has four tabs:

   MATCHES tab:
   - A table of your top matched jobs, sorted best to worst
   - Columns (left to right): rank, match %, ghost risk %,
     posting age with freshness label, job title, company,
     location
   - Match % and ghost % are color-coded:
     * Match: green (50%+), yellow (35-49%), red (<35%)
     * Ghost: green (<20%), yellow (20-59%), orange (60%+)
     * Age: green (<14d), yellow (14-44d), orange (45-89d),
       red (90d+, labeled "Stale")
   - Click any row in the "Inspect a match" dropdown to see:
     * Full job description with link to original posting
     * Section-by-section match breakdown (title, skills,
       summary, experience, lexical) with color-coded scores
     * Which of your skills appear in the posting
     * Your skills not mentioned in this posting
     * If flagged: reasons why it looks suspicious

   RESUME INSIGHTS tab:
   - Missing keywords: terms that appear in your top matches
     but not on your resume (consider adding them)
   - Strong keywords: skills already well-aligned
   - Section advice: specific suggestions per resume section
   - Rephrase hints: concrete wording improvements
   - Common themes: patterns across your target roles

   COMPANY GRADES tab:
   - Each matched company gets a letter grade (A through F)
   - Grades reflect the company's FULL posting history in the
     corpus, not just the jobs that matched your resume
   - Based on: flagged posting rate, median days active,
     repost rate, total posting volume
   - Companies that mass-repost identical descriptions or
     keep stale listings get lower grades

   SYSTEM METRICS tab:
   - This run: rank time, candidates searched, jobs flagged
   - Corpus health: boards attempted, feed uptime, cache stats
   - Run history showing past analyses
   - Engine info: SGM model size, classifier status


UNDERSTANDING THE RATINGS
----------------------------------------------------------------
Match % reflects how well the job fits your resume, adjusted
for posting freshness:
- Recent postings (<7 days old) get a small boost (+5%)
- Normal postings (7-29 days) are scored at face value
- Aging postings (30-59 days) get a small penalty (-5%)
- Old postings (60-89 days) get a larger penalty (-12%)
- Stale postings (90+ days) get a significant penalty (-20%)

This means a stale job needs to be a genuinely better match
to outrank a fresh one. A 40% match posted yesterday is worth
more than a 45% match that has been open for 4 months.


WHAT IS GHOST DETECTION?
----------------------------------------------------------------
A "ghost" job posting is one that is not a real open position.
Common signs include:
- The same description posted dozens of times across boards
- Vague language with no specific technical requirements
- Postings that have been active for hundreds of days
- Companies with extremely high repost rates

The ghost risk percentage estimates how likely a posting is to
be fake or stale. Postings with high ghost scores are colored
orange in the results table. You can adjust the sensitivity
in the sidebar under "3. Settings".


ADJUSTING SETTINGS
----------------------------------------------------------------
In the sidebar under "3. Settings":

- Results to show: How many matches to display (5 to 50)
- Fake-job sensitivity: The cutoff for flagging a job as
  suspicious. A colored label under the slider shows the
  current mode (e.g. "Balanced", "Strict", "Lenient")
- Advanced > Rerank depth: How many top candidates from the
  initial search get a deep comparison to your resume. All
  53,000+ jobs are searched instantly via the TF-IDF index;
  this controls how many get the thorough SGM analysis.
  Default of 150 is a good balance.


REFRESHING THE JOB CORPUS (local only)
----------------------------------------------------------------
The job corpus comes pre-built with 53,000+ jobs. To get
the latest postings, click "Refresh corpus now" in the sidebar
under "Corpus". This runs a background crawl of all Greenhouse
and Lever boards that takes 1 to 2 minutes.

On the hosted version (teamnarou.streamlit.app), the corpus
auto-refreshes every Monday and Thursday via a scheduled
GitHub Action. No manual refresh is needed.


STOPPING THE APP (local only)
----------------------------------------------------------------
To stop Narou, go to the command prompt window that opened when
you double-clicked run.bat and press Ctrl+C. Then close the
window.


TROUBLESHOOTING
----------------------------------------------------------------

"install.bat says Python is not found"
   Install Python 3.10+ from python.org. During installation,
   check the box that says "Add Python to PATH". Then run
   install.bat again.

"install.bat fails to install packages"
   Check your internet connection. If you are behind a corporate
   proxy, you may need to configure pip's proxy settings.

"ERROR: No matching distribution found for streamlit..."
   This means your pip's index cache is stale OR your Python
   is older than 3.10.

   Fix 1 (most common): delete the .venv folder and run the
   installer again. The installer force-clears the pip cache.

   Fix 2: check your Python version. Open a terminal and run:
       python --version
   If it shows 3.9.x or older, install Python 3.10+ from
   python.org and re-run the installer.

   Fix 3 (corporate networks): your IT may force pip through an
   internal mirror that is out of date. Try from a personal
   network, or ask IT to refresh the PyPI mirror.

"The browser shows an error page"
   Wait 10 seconds and refresh. Streamlit sometimes takes a
   moment to start on the first launch.

"Analyze button says corpus is empty"
   The corpus may still be loading. Wait a moment and try again.
   If running locally, check if the data/jobs.sqlite file exists.
   If not, the seed decompression may have failed.

"Results take a long time on first click"
   The first Analyze after launching the app builds a search
   index from the corpus. This takes 1 to 2 minutes depending
   on corpus size. Subsequent analyses are fast (1 to 3 seconds).

"Loading match index takes forever"
   This is the one-time index build over the full corpus. It
   runs once per app launch and is cached after that. On slower
   machines it can take up to 3 minutes. Be patient.

DISCLAIMER
----------------------------------------------------------------
Narou is designed to assist job seekers in navigating job
opportunities, not to make decisions on their behalf. Results
are generated using automated matching and should be treated
as guidance only. Fraud and ghost-job scores are statistical
estimates, not guarantees. Final decisions should always depend
on your own judgment and independent verification of each
opportunity.
